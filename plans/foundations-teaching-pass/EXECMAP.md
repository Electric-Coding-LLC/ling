# Foundations Teaching Pass

## Goal

Make every station on the Foundations spine teach the learner what its complete branch covers in a concise instructor voice.

## Guardrails

- Preserve the current network topology, route accessibility, practice surfaces, progress semantics, navigation and visual language.
- Cover every station currently present on each branch, using `VISION.md` and the curriculum modules as the branch contract.
- Teach the language or practical capability directly; keep product instructions and route-order disclaimers out of the teaching copy.
- Keep every station independently enterable; explanations may show conceptual relationships without implying prerequisites or locks.
- Do not add stations, audio, dependencies, schema changes, delivery work or unrelated copy cleanup.
