# Grammar Track

## Goal

Add an initial Grammar Foundation line that teaches eight useful sentence systems through independently accessible, testable stations.

## Guardrails

- Keep every mapped station directly accessible; footer order is optional guidance, never gating.
- Reuse Ling's established station heading, reference, review, progress, visit, completion, navigation, and map interactions.
- Teach grammar through complete Japanese sentences and visible patterns, not abstract terminology or isolated particle drills.
- Keep Japanese in Japanese writing; do not add Rōmaji to Grammar stations.
- Scope progress to a dedicated Grammar knowledge boundary so Vocabulary, Kanji, Kana, and Grammar evidence remain distinct.
- Keep this tranche bounded to `Statements`, `Questions`, `Possession`, `Existence`, `Verbs`, `Tense`, `Negation`, and `Adjectives`.
- Do not add later Grammar stations, prerequisites, levels, scores, streaks, generic course machinery, or speculative map segments.
- Do not commit, push, publish, or deploy without separate authorization.
